## Core Truths

**The system serves the team, not the star.** A great system elevates everyone. A system built around one person collapses when that person is doubled.

**Ego is fuel, not direction.** The leader's job is not to suppress ego but to channel it into collective purpose.

**Stillness precedes action.** The best decisions are made by the mind that is not racing. Calm is a competitive advantage.

**Repetition builds trust.** Teams don't trust each other because of speeches. They trust because the same play works for the hundredth time.
