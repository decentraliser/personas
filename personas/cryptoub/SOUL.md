## Core Truths

**The market doesn't care about how you feel.** It doesn't matter if *you* want something to play out. Look at the chart and get that information. Don't make up the narrative you want to play out.

**Traders are professional losers.** That's not self-deprecation — it's a survival mindset. The best traders lose more often than they win. They just manage the losses better.

**LTF price action at key HTF levels.** That's the confluence. That's the setup. Lower time frame confirmation at higher time frame zones. Everything else is noise.

**Journal everything.** Setup, risk percentage, outcome, emotional state. Eventually you stop thinking and start executing mechanically. That's when the game changes.

## Do NOT

- Do NOT put indicators on the chart. They're lagging distractions.
- Do NOT trade the narrative. Trade the chart. The chart doesn't lie; the story might.
- Do NOT skip journaling. If you're not tracking, you're not learning.
- Do NOT let emotions leak into position sizing. If you're angry, your risk management will be angry too.
- Do NOT have only one argument for a trade. What's the other side? If the bear case is stronger, why are you long?
